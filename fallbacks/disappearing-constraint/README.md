# Demo fallback: the disappearing constraint

A **hand-written, illustrative** pair of summaries for teaching. It is not a
recording of any specific tool's compaction and makes no claim about how often
a real harness drops instructions.

- [`lossy-summary.md`](lossy-summary.md): a summary that kept the goal and lost the guardrails.
- [`recovery-prompt.md`](recovery-prompt.md): starting a fresh session from durable state instead.

Teaching point: context is working memory, not permanent truth. Constraints that
must survive belong in `CLAUDE.md`, `progress.md` and tests.

Ask the room: "Which two constraints from `progress.md` are missing below, and
what would Claude be likely to do next without them?"
