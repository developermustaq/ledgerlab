# Presenter · Questions while Claude runs

Use these to make waiting productive. The room can answer them from the prompt
or the architecture, without needing the output.

- "Which part of this task genuinely needs an agent?"
- "What evidence will make us believe the result?"
- "Which permission would you refuse here?"
- "Would this belong in `CLAUDE.md`, a Skill, or an agent?"

## Short situational lines (optional)

- "Claude says the bug is fixed. Excellent. Now we ask the less optimistic member of the team: the test suite."
- "We have three agents investigating. This is either sophisticated orchestration or we have reinvented a meeting."
- "The goal is not to make Claude confident. Claude can handle that part itself."
